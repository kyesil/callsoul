Missed calls get one notification per caller, showing how many times they called and how long it rang. The incoming call notification shows the contact's labels. After a call from an unknown number you can label it or add it to contacts. The spam question is a single step, and a number you report as spam is blocked (can be turned off in Settings > Spam). Blocked numbers show as blocked on their page. Automatic spam blocking now needs 5 votes by default.
